Dungeon definitions for Hengband. These govern the 21 dungeons the player can descend into from locations on the wilderness (overworld) map. Each dungeon specifies its depth range (min/max floor, where one floor = 50 feet), the minimum player level required to enter, restrictions on which monster kinds spawn, the kinds of monster nests and pits that can generate, floor and wall terrain, and flags controlling level generation. "Angband" (鉄獄) is the main dungeon of the game, running to a maximum of floor 127 (6350 feet). Dungeons with a final_floor place a guardian boss and/or fixed artifact/item on their deepest level. wild_y/wild_x are the entrance coordinates on the wilderness map.
